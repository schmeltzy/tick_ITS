This repository includes R scripts to recreate analyses for a manuscript entitled "Mycobiota of black-legged tick *Ixodes scapularis* from managed and unmanaged habitats with emphasis on entomopathogens" currently in preparation. In this manuscript we explore the effect of land management treatments on free-living *I. scapularis* ticks by sampling across multiple habitats, life stages, and seasons. Additionally, we characterized the prevalence of entomopathogens known to affect tick populations to evaluate the potential for novel pilot biocontrol studies in future Integrative Tick Management programs.
