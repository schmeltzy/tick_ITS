This repository includes R scripts to recreate analyses for the manuscript "Habitat types and their management affect the mycobiota of medically important tick *Ixodes scapularis*" currently in preparation. In this manuscript we explore the effect of land management treatments on free-living *I. scapularis* ticks by sampling across multiple habitats, life stages, and seasons. Additionally, we characterized the prevalence of entomopathogens known to affect tick populations to evaluate the potential for novel pilot biocontrol studies in future Integrative Tick Management programs.
