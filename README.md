This repository includes R scripts to recreate analyses for a manuscript entitled "Mycobiota of black-legged tick Ixodes scapularis from managed and unmanaged habitats with emphasis on entomopathogens" currently in preparation. In this manuscript we explore the effect of land management treatments on free-living Ixodes scapularis ticks by sampling across multiple habitats, life stages, and seasons. Additionally, we characterized the prevalence of known entomopathogens to evaluate the potential for novel biocontrol pilot studies in future Integrative Tick Management programs.
